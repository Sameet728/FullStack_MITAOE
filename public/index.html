07 Final Product Reveal

The AUREUM
Chronograde I.

Introducing the first watch in the AUREUM Collection — a fusion of traditional Swiss-inspired horology with the sensibility of contemporary design. Built for those who refuse to compromise.

Not a timepiece. An experience. A philosophy. A declaration of intent worn on the wrist.

  • Calibre A-3135 automatic movement, 28,800 vph
  • 42mm 316L stainless steel case with satin and polish finish
  • Domed sapphire crystal with dual anti-reflective coating
  • 200m water resistance with triple-lock screw-down crown
  • 72-hour power reserve with exhibition caseback
  • Five-link Oyster bracelet with Glidelock extension
